# KAMU PILIH: story bible (Season 1 "Kamar 13")

## Premise
Second-person horror. "Kamu" just moved into kos room 13. At 02.13 something knocks and speaks in
the voice of the people you love (first: ibu, 300 km away in the village). Viewers vote A/B in
comments; the majority decides the next part.

## Rules of the entity (never break these)
1. It copies voices perfectly, but it is always a beat late or repeats the last words (the "echo" tell).
2. It cannot open a door from outside. It must be let in: a door opened, a "masuk" answered, a call accepted.
   (So "the knob turns by itself" means it is TESTING the lock, not opening it.)
3. Its time is 02.13. Clocks freeze or glitch at 02.13; every part happens between 02.13 and 03.00.
4. It knows what you send and say on your phone. Calls to it connect for 00:00 seconds.
5. Real ibu is real and alive in the village. The entity is "the previous tenant of room 13" (Part 1:
   "penghuni sebelumnya pindah mendadak"), but that is revealed only in the finale.
6. No gore, no faces. Show it only as: shadows in the door gap, a pale faceless figure (house plate),
   voices, the phone outside the door, the knob.

## Season arc (7 parts; the finale can be pushed to 8–9 if votes stay strong)
| part | beat of the arc |
|---|---|
| 1 | knock + ibu's voice at 02.13 (done) |
| 2 | B: you call ibu; her ringtone rings behind YOUR door (done) |
| 3 | it reveals it can talk through your phone / tests the lock |
| 4 | escalation: it gets something of yours (your voice, your contacts, your photo) |
| 5 | outside help fails (ibu kos, a friend, security): they hear it too, or it uses their voice |
| 6 | the truth: the previous tenant, why they left, what they let in |
| 7 | finale: the last choice decides if you survive the night (choices can end the series here only) |
Season 2 can start in a new place (rumah ibu? asrama?) with the same entity rules.

## Writing rules for every episode
- Write a `hook`: the scariest reveal of the episode as a teaser line (the first thing viewers see and hear).
- 14–18 story beats, 60–90 s total. Open on the vote result (engine does it), then straight into consequence.
- One clear twist per part, and it must follow from the winning choice.
- Each beat line ≤ 45 chars, lowercase, natural spoken Indonesian ("kamu", "nggak", "udah").
- At least one quiet beat (`dur` 2.4–2.8, short text: "hening.", "lima menit.") before the scare.
- Exactly one dead-silence scare at the end of the story (event `silence@end`), usually a question line in red.
- Use at least 3 different shots; never more than ~15 s on the same phone screen.
- Choices: both options plausible, both risky, opposite instincts (act vs hide, call vs stay silent). No "trivia" choices.
  Before Part 7 neither option may end the story; consequences escalate instead.
- Keep a running list of facts the audience saw (below) and never contradict it.

  "ibu · panggilan keluar · 00:00" at 02.15: the call never connected. The phone outside your door was glowing; its light died.
  Choice for Part 4: A = telepon ibu lagi, sekarang / B = buka pintunya, lihat sendiri.

  behind you, something whispered in YOUR voice: "jangan diangkat." (02.31)
  Choice for Part 5: A = angkat teleponnya / B = balik badan, lihat ke kamar.
